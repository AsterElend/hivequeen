      I devote this section to the magical and mysterious items I might encounter in my studies.

      Great Spell Synthesis

      If all of the great spells are contained in Nature, than a sufficiently complicated thinking engine should be able to precipitate out the correct patterns, given inspirations to work from. A bee should be perfect for this, for the mind of a bee is already only a fragment of a greater whole.


      Clockwork Hive

      Tossing the items of inspiration around the hive and pressing a large scroll to it will command the engine to begin it's work.

      All bees are simply extensions of the same being, The Swarm.

          Media

          All Spectrum gemstone shards and powders can now be used as media! A powder is worth one-fifth its corresponding shard.


          The three base shards are all equal. An Onyx shard is worth a shard of quenched allay, and a Moonstone shard is worth a charged amethyst.


          In addition to other gems being used as media, Sympathetic Extraction will cost a negligible amount if the provided item has Curse of the Void.
